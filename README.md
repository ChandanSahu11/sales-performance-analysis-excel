# AtliQ Hardware – Sales & Financial Performance Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview

This is my first Excel analytics project, based on the AtliQ Hardware business case. I analysed sales and financial performance across customers, products, countries, divisions, and fiscal periods from 2019 to 2021.

The objective was to organise business data into clear reports and identify patterns in revenue, product performance, and profitability.

**Project type:** Excel Data Analysis & Reporting  
**Period covered:** 2019–2021 (depending on the report)  
**Currency:** USD  
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

## Business Questions

- How did net sales change between 2019 and 2021?
- Which divisions contributed most to sales growth?
- Which customers generated the highest net sales?
- Which countries contributed the most sales in 2021?
- Which products had the highest sales and quantities sold?
- How did the listed new products perform in 2021?
- How did net sales, COGS, gross margin, and gross margin percentage change over time?

## Analysis and Key Insights

Each finding below links directly to the PDF report used to review it.

### 1. Annual Financial Performance

- Net sales increased from **$87.5M in 2019** to **$196.7M in 2020**, reaching **$598.9M in 2021**.
- Gross margin percentage decreased from **41.43% in 2019** to **37.28% in 2020** and **36.43% in 2021**.
- This shows strong sales growth alongside a decline in gross margin percentage, which deserves further investigation.

**Report:** [View P&L by Fiscal Years](P%26L%20Years.pdf)

### 2. Monthly and Quarterly Profitability

- Reviewed monthly net sales, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross margin, and gross margin percentage across fiscal quarters.
- The report shows the monthly and quarterly movement of these financial metrics and allows comparisons across fiscal years.

**Report:** [View P&L by Fiscal Months](P%26L%20months.pdf)

### 3. Division-wise Sales Performance

- Total sales increased from **$196.7M in 2020** to **$598.9M in 2021**.
- The PC division recorded the highest reported growth at **313.7%**, followed by P&A at **221.5%** and N&S at **84.4%**.

**Report:** [View Division Level Analysis](Division.pdf)

### 4. Customer Net Sales Performance

- Amazon recorded the highest 2021 net sales at **$82.1M**.
- AtliQ Exclusive generated **$61.1M**, while AtliQ e Store generated **$53.0M** in 2021.
- The report compares customer sales across 2019, 2020, and 2021.

**Report:** [View Customer Net Sales Performance](NetSales.pdf)

### 5. Country-wise Sales Performance

- India was the leading country in the 2021 report, with **$161.3M** in sales.
- The USA followed with **$87.8M**, while South Korea recorded **$49.0M**.
- The five countries shown together contributed **$367.2M**.

**Report:** [View Top 5 Countries](Top%20countries.pdf)

### 6. Top 10 Products by Sales

- The listed top 10 products recorded combined sales of **$52.0M in 2021**, compared with **$6.4M in 2020**.
- AQ Electron 4 3600 Desktop Processor recorded **$19.4M** in 2021 sales.
- AQ Smash 2 recorded **$11.2M** in 2021 sales.

**Report:** [View Top 10 Products](Top%2010%20products.pdf)

### 7. Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products by Quantity

- Compared the five products with the highest quantities sold against the five products with the lowest quantities sold.
- AQ Master wired x1 Ms appears at the top of the high-quantity list with **4.2M units**.
- AQ HOME Allin1 Gen 2 appears in the bottom-five list with **8.9K units**.

**Report:** [View Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products](Top%205%20and%20Bottom%205%20products.pdf)

### 8. New Product Performance in 2021

- The report lists 2021 sales for the products included in the new-product analysis.
- The listed products generated a combined **$176.2M**.
- AQ Qwerty recorded the highest sales in this report at **$22.0M**.

**Report:** [View New Products – 2021](New%20products-2021.pdf)

## Limitations and Future Improvements

- This repository currently contains PDF exports. The original editable Excel workbook is not included, so the underlying formulas, PivotTable configuration, and interactive filter behaviour cannot be independently inspected.
- Some exported reports contain unnecessary blank pages. I plan to improve the print layout and export settings.
- The quantity report should use a quantity-specific unit label rather than the general USD label.
- The annual P&L report's `21 vs 20` values should be labelled clearly as percentage-of-prior-year or percentage growth, to avoid ambiguity.
- Future improvements could include an executive summary dashboard, clearer KPI definitions, and business recommendations supported by the underlying data.
